How it works

Three steps, no account login.

1
Request your Instagram export

In Instagram, open Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Export your information. Choose JSON and include Followers and Following.

2
Choose the JSON files

Find followers_1.json and following.json in the downloaded export folder. File names can vary.

3
Compare the lists

FollowBack finds non-mutuals, mutuals, and accounts you don't follow back.
